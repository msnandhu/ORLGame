    Driving

    W / S throttle / brake (hold S when stopped to reverse)
    A / D steer · Space handbrake
    C driving mode: Normal / Sport / Off-road / Rock crawl / Snow / Mud (road cars: Normal / Sport / Snow)
    G transfer case 2H → 4H → 4L · L diff lock (off-roaders only)
    T auto / manual · X / Z shift up / down (manual)
    F hold at any fuel pump to refuel (paid) · N headlights and driving lamps
    R recover vehicle (upright) · E exit
    K use a repair kit (stopped) · F in the camp shed: paid workshop repair
    U at the camp shed or an island garage: open the garage
    Your vehicles: off-roaders park at camp, road cars in the town car parks. Others there are for sale in the garage.
